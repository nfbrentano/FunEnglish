Fun English is a free collection of interactive activities for teachers of English as a second language: quizzes, flashcards, fill-in-the-blanks, team quiz boards and discussion cards, across grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, speaking, writing, videos, pictures and just-for-fun warm-ups.

## Made for the classroom

Every activity is designed to be **projected** for the whole class: big text, keyboard shortcuts, fullscreen, team scores and timers. When you want students to practise on their own, **share the activity** with a link or a QR code: they open it on their phones, with no account and no distractions.

## Pedagogical Leadership & Teacher Dani

<a id="teacher-dani"></a>

Known to her students as **Teacher Dani**, she actively leads the creation and pedagogical review of our digital educational content, with a dedicated focus on developing **speaking fluency**, **natural pronunciation**, and **listening comprehension**.

### Background and Pedagogical Coordination

Teacher Dani is part of the leadership team at **Skylimit Idiomas**, participating in international technical visits and educational exchanges to enhance and refine the institution's award-winning learning materials.

### Practical Learning Approach

Her teaching philosophy emphasizes real-world communication over rote memorization. She creates practical lessons centered on everyday expressions, adjectives, and specialized vocabulary tailored to daily scenarios:

- Adjectives in English to describe personality
- Practical vocabulary for everyday topics

### Listening & Autonomous Study Tips

Teacher Dani strongly encourages autonomous language practice. She recommends:

- **Media Immersion:** Watching movies and TV series with original English audio and English subtitles for beginners to train auditory comprehension and natural cadence.
- **Dedicated Listening Tools:** Exploring specialized platforms for self-paced listening development, such as ESL English Listening (Cyber Listening Lab) and TalkEnglish.com.

## How the activities are made

Activities and illustrations are drafted with the help of artificial intelligence and then reviewed by experienced educators, ensuring natural English phrasing, pedagogical accuracy, and appropriate CEFR level leveling (from A1 to C1). If you spot a mistake or have suggestions, please [let us know](/contact).

## Free

Fun English is free. A teacher account is optional and only saves your favorites, class lists and recently played activities.

Fun English is a free collection of interactive activities for teachers of English as a second language: quizzes, flashcards, fill-in-the-blanks, team quiz boards and discussion cards, across grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, speaking, writing, videos, pictures and just-for-fun warm-ups.

## Made for the classroom

Every activity is designed to be **projected** for the whole class: big text, keyboard shortcuts, fullscreen, team scores and timers. When you want students to practise on their own, **share the activity** with a link or a QR code: they open it on their phones, with no account and no distractions.

## How the activities are made

Activities and illustrations are drafted with the help of artificial intelligence and then reviewed by a teacher, who checks the English, the answers and the level. If you spot a mistake, please [let us know](/contact) and we'll fix it.

## Free

Fun English is free. A teacher account is optional and only saves your favorites, class lists and recently played activities.

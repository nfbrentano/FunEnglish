Fun English ("we", "the site") is a free collection of interactive English activities for ESL teachers. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to fun-english-972a2.web.app and any future domain of the site.

**Data controller:** Natanael Brentano, Brazil. Contact: [nfgbrentano@gmail.com](mailto:nfgbrentano@gmail.com).

## What we collect

**If you create an account (teachers):**

- **Account data:** your name, email address and, if you sign in with Google, your profile photo.
- **Your activity on the site:** the activities you favorite, the lists you create and the activities you recently played ("Recently played", up to the last 50).

**If you send us a message:** the name, email address, subject and message you type in the contact form.

**If you use the site without an account (including students on a shared link):** we don't collect personal data. Activities opened from a shared student link (`?mode=student`) don't require an account and don't save anything about the student.

**Stored on your device:** your theme choice (in `localStorage`), a favorite you clicked before logging in (in `sessionStorage`, until you log in), and your login session (kept by Firebase Authentication in your browser). We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

## Why we use it

- To create and secure your account and let you log in (legal basis: performance of the service you asked for).
- To save your favorites, lists and recently played activities (same basis).
- To answer the messages you send us (legitimate interest in replying to you).

We don't sell your data, don't use it for advertising and don't build profiles about you.

## Who processes it

- **Google Firebase / Google Cloud** (Authentication, Firestore database and Hosting) stores and serves the data above on our behalf. Google may process it on servers outside Brazil, under its own data protection terms.
- **YouTube** (Google): some activities embed YouTube videos. When a video loads, YouTube may set cookies and collect data under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We don't share your data with anyone else, except when required by law.

## How long we keep it

- Account data, favorites and lists: while your account exists.
- Recently played: the last 50 entries; older ones are deleted automatically.
- Contact messages: up to 12 months after we reply.

## Your rights

Under the Brazilian LGPD and, where it applies, the European GDPR, you can ask to **access**, **correct**, **export** or **delete** your data, and to withdraw consent. To delete your account and all its data, email [nfgbrentano@gmail.com](mailto:nfgbrentano@gmail.com) from the address of your account. We reply within 15 days. You can also complain to Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD) or your local authority.

You can remove a favorite, a list or your theme choice at any time on the site.

## Children

Accounts are meant for teachers (18 or older). Students don't need an account and no data about them is collected through shared links.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Important changes will be announced on the site.
